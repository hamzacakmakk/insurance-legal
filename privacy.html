Privacy Policy

Insurance Exam Prep — last updated: November 2026

There is no account and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, we do not run a server that stores your data, and there are no ads. Your study data stays on your phone.

1. What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The app has no registration and no login. It does not access your contacts, photos or location.

2. What stays on your device

Your answers, results, mistakes, saved questions, chosen licenses, state, exam date, daily study time, reminders and settings are stored only on your phone. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

3. Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple and validated through RevenueCat, which receive an anonymous purchase identifier and your subscription status. We never see your payment details.

4. Usage measurement

The app may record anonymous usage events (for example, which screens are opened and whether a practice exam was finished) to understand which parts of the app help people. These events are not linked to your name, email or any account, and they never include your answers.

5. Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for an insurance license exam and is not directed at children.

6. Your choices

You can reset your progress in Settings and remove all local data by deleting the app. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated before the version that needs the change is released.

8. Contact

For any privacy question, use the support email listed on the app's App Store page.

Insurance Exam Prep is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any state insurance department, the NAIC, Pearson VUE, PSI or Prometric.
